# Product Requirements Document: KEYSTYLE SARTORIAL

## 1. Project Overview
**KEYSTYLE SARTORIAL** is a high-concept, bespoke image consulting platform founded by **Flora Leong**. The brand specifically niches in premium makeovers for middle-aged men, CEOs, and global executives. It bridges the gap between personal narrative and aesthetic mastery through "Intelligent Styling."

---

## 2. The 5W & 1H (Core Brand Story)

### WHO (Identity & Audience)
*   **Founder**: Flora Leong (Multi-disciplinary entrepreneur: Culinary & Image Strategy).
*   **Target**: Middle-aged professional men, high-profile executives, and boardroom leaders.
*   **Persona**: The "Modern Gentleman" who values authority, precision, and substance.

### WHAT (The Offering)
*   Bespoke image strategies, wardrobe refinements, executive gift vouchers, and corporate leadership workshops.
*   Deliverables: Strategic personal branding, authoritative silhouettes, and tactical wardrobes.

### WHERE (Geography & Habitat)
*   Headquartered in Singapore (`flora.com.sg`).
*   Service Scope: Global travel hubs (e.g., London to Hong Kong).
*   Context: High-stakes boardrooms, strategic networking events, and private lifestyle rotations.

### WHEN (The Timeframe)
*   Positioned for the 2024–2026 global executive.
*   Focus: Immediate brand elevation for career pivots, high-profile media appearances, or institutional leadership.

### WHY (The Rationale)
*   **Philosophy**: "Style is a way to say who you are without having to speak."
*   **Strategic Need**: To ensure outward presence is aligned with internal achievements. To command respect before a single word is uttered.

### HOW (The Methodology)
*   **Intelligent Styling**: A proprietary framework intersecting consumer psychology, color science, and bespoke craftsmanship.
*   **Architecture of the Individual**: Analyzing facial structure, body geometry, and professional ecosystem to build tactical wardrobes.

---

## 3. Product Features & Content Sections
*   **Hero Section**: Bold typographic branding (`KEY STYLE`) with high-contrast editorial imagery and cinematic video content.
*   **Hero Vision**: "Redefining the modern professional wardrobe through bespoke curation and sartorial intelligence."
*   **Consultancy Services**:
    *   *Personal Styling*: Deep brand dives.
    *   *Wardrobe Edit*: Systematic collections pruning.
    *   *Corporate Workshops*: Executive presence for leadership teams.
*   **The Journal (Diaries)**: Content-rich MDX posts (The Perfect Power Suit, Global Travel Wardrobes, Bespoke vs MTM).
*   **Social Connectivity**: Curated "Sartorial Vision" via Instagram integration.

## 4. Aesthetic Requirements
*   **Palette**: Emerald (main), Gold/Champagne (`#E5C374`), and Core Black.
*   **Typography**: Manrope (Modern/Strong) and Inter (Clean/Professional).
*   **Vibe**: Dark mode, glassmorphism, editorial, premium, and authoritative.

## Deliverables

### 1. Assessment Questionnaire Form (Google Docs format)
A pre-session intake form clients fill out before the 45-min consultation. Covers:
- Personal info & lifestyle context
- Style self-assessment
- T-shirt word exercise (from Fashion Feng Shui)
- Intention identification
- Wardrobe comfort zone questions
- Current photo upload section

### 2. Results Presentation (Google Slides format)
A slide deck Flora uses DURING the 45-min session to walk clients through their results:
- Welcome & intro to the Elemental Edge system
- Their primary element reveal
- What their element means for style
- Quick wins they can implement today
- Teaser of what deeper transformation looks like (upsell)

### 3. Client Takeaway Report Booklet (Google Docs format)
A personalised report Flora fills in and gives to clients after the session:
- Their element profile summary
- Key style recommendations
- 3 quick wins to implement
- Notes section for Flora's handwritten/typed pointers
- "What's Next" section (upgrade path to Spark/Signature)

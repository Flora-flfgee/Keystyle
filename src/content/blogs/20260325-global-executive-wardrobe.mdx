# Travel Wardrobe for the Global Executive

Curating a 5-piece travel rotation that transitions seamlessly from London to Hong Kong.

## The Versatility of High-Performance Fabrics
For those traveling across continents, the key to a successful wardrobe is versatility. High-twist wools and tech blends offer wrinkle resistance and breathability, crucial for transitioning between varied climates.

## The 5-Piece Rotation
A five-piece travel rotation should include:

- **The Navy Blazer**: The anchor of any wardrobe.
- **The Grey Trousers**: Versatile for both formal and informal settings.
- **Two Crisp White Shirts**: Always professional and easy to match.
- **A Cashmere Knit**: For long flights and layering.

## Seamless Transitions
London and Hong Kong represent different facets of the global business landscape. While the London aesthetic is more traditional, Hong Kong values efficiency and modern cuts. A well-curated wardrobe bridges these two worlds.

- **London**: Classic silhouettes and deeper tones.
- **Hong Kong**: Modern proportions and high-performance textiles.

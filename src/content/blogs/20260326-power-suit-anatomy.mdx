# The Anatomy of the Perfect Power Suit

Why lapel width matters more than brand name in 2024. An exploration of proportion and presence.

## The Foundation of Form
A power suit is not merely a piece of clothing; it is a declaration of intent. The architecture of a suit begins with the shoulders. A well-tailored shoulder provides a silhouette that exudes confidence without being overly structured.

## Why Lapel Width Matters
Many aficionados focus on the brand, yet the lapel width is the most critical factor in achieving a balanced proportion. A 3.5-inch notch or peak lapel remains the gold standard for a timeless, executive presence.

## The Importance of Presence
Presence is not about being noticed; it is about being remembered. The drape of the fabric and the precision of the sleeve length all contribute to the overall aura.

- **Shoulders**: Sharp and defined
- **Lapels**: Proportional and bold
- **Fabric**: Super 150s Wool or Cashmere blend
